You may be wondering... How are these new Pokemon balanced? Well im glad you asked!

I took the stats from the first 5 gens of pokemon games, found the average total stats for the first evolution & final evolution. Found which starter excelled at what and the variation of that between the types. Then I picked what types of attackers I wanted my starters to be based on all of that and me remembering what felt best when playing each game, settling on a fast attacker and 2 balanced pokemon (2 variations of tanky + attacking). Then after finding the median % spread of the 6 stats for fast attackers and balanced starters I applied that to our new starters and their base stats were born!

As of writing this, their base stats are the following:

Kiwi: 47HP 63ATK 51DEF 48SPD 42SP.ATK 62SP.DEF

Sheep: 64HP 47ATK 41DEF 50SPD 64SP.ATK 47SP.DEF

Eel: 41HP 53ATK 41DEF 66SPD 63SP.ATK 50SP.DEF

As you may be able to tell; Kiwi is going to be a SP.DEF tanky Physical Attacker, Sheep will be an HP tanky Special Attacker and Eel will be a Fast Special Attacker!
